# WarEra data sources

WINT reads the public WarEra tRPC API at `https://api2.warera.io/trpc/`. The reproducible anonymous probe is `npm run probe`; its response metadata is saved in [`artifacts/probes/probe-results.json`](artifacts/probes/probe-results.json). The probe ran on 2026-10-11. It stores response shapes, status codes and rate headers, not complete user profiles or article bodies. The official [API documentation](https://api2.warera.io/docs/) and community [endpoint notes](https://github.com/majimawrks/warera-api-docs) informed candidate procedures; [WarEraProjects' Python client](https://github.com/WarEraProjects/api-client-py) supplied candidate pagination signatures. Every enabled procedure below was then checked live without credentials. At probe time, `swagger-ui-init.js` returned HTTP 200 with a zero-byte body, so it could not serve as a schema source.

| WINT source | Verified public procedure | Live shape | Initial polling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| countries | `country.getAllCountries` + `country.getCountryById({countryId})` | 180 records in one discovery array, then individual details | 24 h after sweep, three records per tick |
| regions | `region.getRegionsObject` + `region.getById({regionId})` | 726 discovery records keyed by ID, then individual details | 6 h after sweep, three records per tick |
| players | `user.getUsersByCountry({countryId,limit,cursor})` then `user.getUserLite({userId})` | Country page has IDs and `nextCursor`; lite profile has username, country, level and activity fields | Four profiles per page using one verified tRPC batch; one page per country before rotating |
| parties | `party.getManyPaginated({limit,cursor})` | Full party objects and `nextCursor` | 1 h after full sweep; ten records per page |
| military-units | `mu.getManyPaginated({limit,cursor})` | Full unit objects and `nextCursor` | 1 h after full sweep; ten records per page |
| battles | `battle.getBattles({limit,isActive:true,cursor})` | Active battle objects and `nextCursor` | 2 min after full sweep; ten records per page |
| articles | `article.getArticlesPaginated({type:'last',limit,cursor})` | Article objects and `nextCursor`; `type` is required | Head refresh plus continuous archive traversal; ten candidates per page |
| markets | `itemTrading.getPrices` | Map of 24 global item codes to prices | 10 min; three items per tick |

The individual detail procedures `country.getCountryById`, `region.getById`, `party.getById`, `mu.getById`, `battle.getById`, `article.getArticleById` and `user.getUserLite` also returned HTTP 200 with valid IDs. Each saved row has a `source_url` to its confirmed original API procedure. A POST with an empty JSON body to `itemTrading.getPrices` also returned HTTP 200; ingestion uses GET. Observed anonymous responses carried `ratelimit-limit: 100`, `ratelimit-remaining`, and `ratelimit-reset` headers. This is an observation, not a guaranteed service limit. The client stops early near the reported limit and honors `Retry-After` when present.

Only selected public fields are retained. The article feed can return `isPublic:false` records even anonymously; WINT skips those, unpublished entries and deleted entries. Article body HTML is deliberately omitted because redistribution rights have not been established; the archive stores title, author ID, publication timestamp when present, language, category and counts. A missing publication timestamp remains null. The market price map is global; WINT does not claim country-specific quotes or order-book liquidity from it. Country and region discovery responses are fetched once per sweep. Their ordered IDs are persisted in the cursor, so later ticks hydrate only three details and do not repeatedly parse the large full feeds. IDs and returned detail IDs are validated. Initial coverage still takes multiple cron ticks. Party, unit and battle cursors now continue to the end of the feed. Articles maintain separate live-head and archive progress, without a fixed page ceiling. The player census rotates one page per country with an independent country cursor; completed country sweeps wait six hours before restarting. A row is rewritten and snapshotted only when selected normalized fields change. Snapshot and price samples older than the configured retention window are removed in small batches.

`companies` is seeded in `source_registry` as `unavailable-unverified` and disabled. Its candidate `company.getCompanies` has not been verified live for public listing or a hydration contract. `events` and `alerts` are seeded as disabled `derived-not-implemented` sources for later verified or derived data. No chat, Discord messages, private profiles, or speculative relations are collected. If an enabled endpoint becomes unavailable or requires authorization, its status and last error are recorded while the last valid rows remain queryable.

The worker uses source and global D1 compare-and-set leases. Its scheduled slots prioritize players and articles; GitHub Actions additionally projects public fields from large upstream responses before sending small authenticated pages to the Worker. Both collectors share D1 cursors and the same UTC budget. Batches contain four player profiles for direct Cron runs, eight for compact collection, up to ten paginated candidates or three country/region details. Before fetching, a tick reserves `140 + 10 * rowLimit` writes. Successful captures reconcile that reservation against actual D1 `meta.rows_written`, retaining an eight-write settlement margin; failed or unmeasured reservations remain charged. The daily collection budget is 80,000 writes, leaving account-wide headroom. This does not measure other applications or all administrative writes. Exhausted collection pauses until the next UTC day. The initial discovery of countries/regions and direct profile fetching can still have CPU spikes; compact collection and fallback CPU are measured in VALIDATION.md. Intervals describe pauses after completed sweeps, not guaranteed freshness of every entity.

The anonymous throughput probe on 2026-10-12 verified 20/50-item pages and ordered POST tRPC batches; metadata only is in `artifacts/probes/throughput.json`. Production batches intentionally remain smaller for Worker CPU and D1 budgets. Unknown article visibility is excluded: both `isPublic` and `isPublished` must be true; deleted entries and article bodies are never stored.
