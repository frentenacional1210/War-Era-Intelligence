# WarEra data sources

WINT reads the public WarEra tRPC API at `https://api2.warera.io/trpc/`. The reproducible anonymous probe is `npm run probe`; its response metadata is saved in [`artifacts/probes/probe-results.json`](artifacts/probes/probe-results.json). The probe ran on 2026-10-11. It stores response shapes, status codes and rate headers, not complete user profiles or article bodies. The official [API documentation](https://api2.warera.io/docs/) and community [endpoint notes](https://github.com/majimawrks/warera-api-docs) informed candidate procedures; [WarEraProjects' Python client](https://github.com/WarEraProjects/api-client-py) supplied candidate pagination signatures. Every enabled procedure below was then checked live without credentials. At probe time, `swagger-ui-init.js` returned HTTP 200 with a zero-byte body, so it could not serve as a schema source.

| WINT source | Verified public procedure | Live shape | Initial polling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| countries | `country.getAllCountries` | 180 full records in one array | 24 h, three records per tick |
| regions | `region.getRegionsObject` | 726 records keyed by ID | 6 h, three records per tick |
| players | `user.getUsersByCountry({countryId,limit,cursor})` then `user.getUserLite({userId})` | Country page has IDs and `nextCursor`; lite profile has username, country, level and activity fields | 1 h; one profile per tick, countries rotated |
| parties | `party.getManyPaginated({limit,cursor})` | Full party objects and `nextCursor` | 1 h; three records per tick |
| military-units | `mu.getManyPaginated({limit,cursor})` | Full unit objects and `nextCursor` | 1 h; three records per tick |
| battles | `battle.getBattles({limit,isActive:true,cursor})` | Active battle objects and `nextCursor` | 2 min; three records per tick |
| articles | `article.getArticlesPaginated({type:'last',limit,cursor})` | Article objects and `nextCursor`; `type` is required | 5 min; three records per tick |
| markets | `itemTrading.getPrices` | Map of 24 global item codes to prices | 10 min; three items per tick |

The individual detail procedures `country.getCountryById`, `region.getById`, `party.getById`, `mu.getById`, `battle.getById`, `article.getArticleById` and `user.getUserLite` also returned HTTP 200 with valid IDs. Each saved row has a `source_url` to its confirmed original API procedure. A POST with an empty JSON body to `itemTrading.getPrices` also returned HTTP 200; ingestion uses GET. Observed anonymous responses carried `ratelimit-limit: 100`, `ratelimit-remaining`, and `ratelimit-reset` headers. This is an observation, not a guaranteed service limit. The client stops early near the reported limit and honors `Retry-After` when present.

Only selected public fields are retained. The article feed can return `isPublic:false` records even anonymously; WINT skips those, unpublished entries and deleted entries. Article body HTML is deliberately omitted because redistribution rights have not been established; the archive stores title, author ID, publication timestamp when present, language, category and counts. A missing publication timestamp remains null. The market price map is global; WINT does not claim country-specific quotes or order-book liquidity from it. Country and region full responses are processed in slices, so initial coverage takes multiple cron ticks. Party, unit, battle and article cursors are capped per sweep and return to the newest page. The player census advances country by country and will be incomplete during initial collection. A row is rewritten and snapshotted only when selected normalized fields change. Snapshot and price samples older than the configured retention window are removed in small batches.

`companies` is seeded in `source_registry` as `unavailable-unverified` and disabled. Its candidate `company.getCompanies` has not been verified live for public listing or a hydration contract. `events` and `alerts` are seeded as disabled `derived-not-implemented` sources for later verified or derived data. No chat, Discord messages, private profiles, or speculative relations are collected. If an enabled endpoint becomes unavailable or requires authorization, its status and last error are recorded while the last valid rows remain queryable.

The worker uses source and global D1 compare-and-set leases, at most one due source per cron tick, one player detail or three other records, and short error backoff. It stays within a conservative bound of 28 combined API and D1 subrequests even if every API call retries twice. Before fetching, a tick reserves 140 *estimated* D1 writes in a UTC daily counter. The ingestion cap is 60,000 reserved writes per day, leaving headroom under the free tier for visitor queries and administration. This is a conservative budget guard, not a count of billed writes. When exhausted, ingestion reports `quota-paused` and resumes the next UTC day while cached panels remain readable. The source registry, budget table and run log expose freshness and errors to the API. Old run and budget rows are cleaned in small batches. The initial deployment used larger batches; they were reduced after measuring production CPU. The intervals describe the pause after a sweep, not guaranteed freshness of every entity: partial pages resume after sixty seconds and compete with other due sources and the daily budget.
